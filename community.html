Community Guidelines

Yumble is for people who love food and want genuine connections. Keep it kind, real, and safe.

Be genuine

Use your real name, real photos, and be honest about who you are. Every member is verified with a selfie and ID to keep Yumble authentic.

Be respectful

Treat others how you'd want to be treated. No harassment, hate speech, discrimination, threats, or unsolicited explicit content. If someone isn't interested, move on graciously.

Be safe

Protect your privacy—don't share financial details or personal addresses too soon. Meet in public places, tell a friend your plans, and trust your instincts. Report anything that feels wrong.

Keep it legal & appropriate

No content involving minors, no illegal activity, no solicitation or spam. Yumble is strictly for adults aged 18+.

Reporting

If a member breaks these guidelines, use the in-app report option or email safety@yumble.app. We review reports and may warn, suspend, or remove accounts.
